# Builder & Contributor Opportunities

Understanding subnets is the start. The real value comes from **participating**. Here are the paths open to you after the camp, roughly from lowest to highest commitment.

## Run a Miner

The most direct entry point: deploy a miner and start earning emissions for real work. You'll do this hands-on for **SN13** and **SN41**. Once comfortable, the same pattern transfers to almost any subnet.

## Become a Validator

Validators score miner work and help secure the network. Running a validator demands more stake and uptime, but it's central to a subnet's economics: and a deeper way to learn how incentive mechanisms behave.

## Build & Own a Subnet

The highest-leverage path: define your own specialized market, design its incentive mechanism, and register a NetUID. If you spot a commodity the network doesn't yet produce well, this is where you create it.

## Contribute to Subnet Repos

Most subnets are open source. Fixing bugs, improving miner/validator tooling, writing docs, or proposing scoring improvements is a strong way to build reputation and get close to subnet teams: often a precursor to grants or core roles.

## Join the Community

Plug into the Bittensor and subnet-specific Discords, follow the teams (Macrocosmos, Taoshi, and others), and learn in public. Most opportunities surface through community channels before anywhere else.

## Apply for Opentensor Foundation Grants

The **Opentensor Foundation (OTF)** funds builders advancing the ecosystem: new subnets, tooling, research, and education. If you have a concrete project, a grant can fund the work.
