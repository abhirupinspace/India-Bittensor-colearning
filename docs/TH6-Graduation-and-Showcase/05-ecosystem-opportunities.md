# Ecosystem Opportunities

Graduation isn't the finish line — it's your on-ramp into the Bittensor ecosystem. Here's where to go next.

## Keep Building

- **Keep mining** — your miner already works; optimize strategy, climb the rankings, add a second subnet
- **Become a validator** — run a validator to help score miners and earn validation emissions
- **Build a subnet** — have an idea for a new incentive mechanism? Spin up your own subnet
- See the [Resources](/resources) page for repos, dashboards, and deeper guides

## Grants & Funding

- **OTF (Opentensor Foundation) grants** — funding for subnets, tooling, and ecosystem work
- Subnet teams often fund miners, contributors, and integrations directly
- Bring your camp write-up and running miner as proof of execution

## Stay Connected

- Join the **alumni / community** channels from the camp
- Follow subnet Discords for changelogs and registration windows
- Share your work — the people who stay visible get the next opportunity
