# Top Contributors Recognition

We recognize participants who went above and beyond — not just the highest score, but the people who built, helped, and shared.

## Recognition Categories

- **Top Miner Score** — highest sustained incentive / rank on a subnet (SN13 score)
- **Best Write-Up** — clearest, most useful submission write-up others can learn from
- **Most Helpful** — answered questions, unblocked peers, shared fixes in the community
- **Best Build** — most creative or polished miner, strategy, or tooling on top of a subnet

## How Winners Are Picked

- **Score categories** — verified on-chain via `btcli subnets metagraph` and taostats
- **Community categories** — organizers + peer nominations during the camp
- **Tie-breaks** — favor reproducibility and how much others learned from the work
- Winners are announced at the **Graduation Ceremony**
