# Program Structure, Learning Track & Quests

> A quick map of how the **HackQuest × Bittensor Co-Learning Camp #23 India** is organized, so you always know where you are and what to do next.

## The 6 Townhalls

The camp runs as **six Townhalls (TH1 → TH6)**:

- **TH1 — Foundations & Introduction**: Web3, AI, decentralized AI, and what Bittensor is
- **TH2 — Tooling & Ecosystem**: network structure, incentives, btcli, tokenomics
- **TH3 — Core Subnets**: the subnets you'll actually work with
- **TH4 — Building**: local mining and your first real miner
- **TH5 — Guided Projects**: shipping a miner on a live subnet (Data Universe, SN13)
- **TH6 — Graduation**: final submission, next steps, and joining the community

## Learning Track

Each Townhall pairs **learning content** (the pages in that section) with a **hands-on quest**. Read the content first, then apply it. Concepts build on each other, so work through the Townhalls in order rather than jumping ahead.

## Quests & Submissions

Every Townhall ends with a **quest** — a small, concrete deliverable (a wallet, a running node, a screenshot, a short write-up). You complete the quest and **submit** it to mark that Townhall done. Quests are how you prove progress and earn your place toward graduation.

## How to Progress

Move **Townhall by Townhall**: finish the content, complete the quest, submit, then advance. New here and want the fast track? Start with the **Overview** for the big picture, and use the **One-Shot Guide** to go from zero to a running miner quickly.
